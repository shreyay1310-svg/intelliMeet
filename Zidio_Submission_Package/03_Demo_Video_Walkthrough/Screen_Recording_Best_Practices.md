# 🎥 Screen Recording Best Practices & Setup Guide

Follow this guide to produce a clear, crisp, professional screen recording for your Zidio submission.

---

## 🛠️ Recommended Recording Software (Free Options)

1. **Loom (Easiest & Cloud-Hosted):**
   - Download the free desktop app or Chrome extension at [loom.com](https://www.loom.com).
   - Allows recording screen + webcam circle in corner.
   - Automatically gives you an instant shareable link.

2. **OBS Studio (Highest Quality, Free & Open Source):**
   - Download from [obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com).
   - Canvas Resolution: `1920x1080` (1080p).
   - Output Framerate: 30 fps or 60 fps.
   - Format: MP4 or MKV.

3. **Windows Built-in Game Bar (Quickest, No Install Needed):**
   - Press `Win + Alt + R` on Windows to start recording instantly.
   - Press `Win + Alt + R` again to stop.
   - Video saved in `C:\Users\<user>\Videos\Captures`.

---

## 📋 Pre-Recording Checklist

- [ ] **Close Personal Tabs:** Close WhatsApp Web, email inboxes, personal bookmarks bar (`Ctrl + Shift + B` in Chrome to hide bookmarks).
- [ ] **Clean Desktop / Window:** Put the browser in full screen or maximize window.
- [ ] **Zoom Level:** Set browser zoom to 100% (or 110% if your screen is 4K so text is easily readable).
- [ ] **Microphone Check:** Test a 10-second audio snippet to ensure there is no fan noise or distortion.
- [ ] **Prepare Accounts:** Keep `employee@intellimeet.io` / `Password123!` and `admin@intellimeet.io` / `AdminPass123!` handy on a notepad.
- [ ] **Camera / Mic Permissions:** Test that browser permissions for camera and mic are pre-approved so no blocking popups appear unexpectedly during recording.

---

## 📤 Uploading & Setting Permissions

After recording your 3–7 minute video:
1. **YouTube:** Upload video -> Set Visibility to **"Unlisted"** (evaluators can view with the link, but it won't be indexed publicly on your channel).
2. **Google Drive:** Upload MP4 -> Right click -> Share -> Change General Access to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
3. Copy the URL and paste it into `01_Final_Submission_Form/Final_Submission_Form.md`.
