# 💡 Feedback Video — Recording Tips & Setup

A high-quality feedback video leaves a lasting positive impression on evaluators. Follow these simple tips:

---

## 📱 Device & Camera Placement
- **Device:** Your smartphone selfie camera (1080p, 30fps) or laptop webcam.
- **Angle:** Keep the camera at **eye level**. Avoid looking down into the camera. Stack a couple of books under your laptop if necessary.
- **Orientation:** Landscape mode (horizontal) is preferred, but vertical format is also acceptable.

---

## 💡 Lighting & Audio
- **Lighting:** Sit facing a window or a lamp. Light should fall directly on your face, not behind you (avoid backlighting).
- **Audio:** Use wired earphones, AirPods, or a lapel mic to eliminate background echo. Turn off noisy fans/AC during recording.
- **Background:** Choose a clean, quiet wall, study desk, or minimal room background.

---

## 🗣️ Delivery & Presence
- **Eye Contact:** Look straight into the camera lens, not at your own face on the screen.
- **Pacing:** Speak naturally and at a moderate speed. Don't rush; pause briefly between points.
- **Smile:** Start and end with a warm, confident smile.

---

## 📤 Sharing Link
- Upload to **YouTube (Unlisted)** or **Google Drive ("Anyone with link can view")**.
- Verify the link opens in an incognito window before pasting into the submission form.
