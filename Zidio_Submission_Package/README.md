## 🎯 Quick Execution Steps for Submission

1. **Step 1: Record Demo Video (3–7 mins)**  
   Open `03_Demo_Video_Walkthrough/Demo_Video_Spoken_Script_English.md` (or Hinglish).  
   Follow the on-screen cues and record your screen while reading the script.  
   Upload the video to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Google Drive with "Anyone with link can view").

2. **Step 2: Record Feedback Video (1–2 mins)**  
   Open `04_Feedback_Video/Feedback_Video_Script_and_Guide.md`.  
   Record yourself on camera speaking the reflection script.  
   Upload as **Unlisted** or public link.

3. **Step 3: Live Deployment (Optional / If submitting live link)**  
   Follow `05_Live_Deployment_Guide/Step_by_Step_Deployment_Guide.md` to deploy frontend to Vercel and backend to Render.

4. **Step 4: Fill Final Submission Form**  
   Open `01_Final_Submission_Form/Final_Submission_Form.md`, paste your video links and repo link, and copy the text directly into the Zidio submission dashboard.
